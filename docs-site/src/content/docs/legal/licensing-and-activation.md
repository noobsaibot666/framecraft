title: Licensing & Activation
description: How the direct-download trial, purchase, and activation work.
---

This page applies to the **direct-download version** of Framecraft (purchased from the website, not the Mac App Store). If you bought it from the Mac App Store, Apple handles your purchase and license — there's nothing to activate.

## Trial

The direct-download build starts a 14-day free trial the first time you launch it, with full functionality. No account or payment is required to start it.

## Purchase and activation

After purchasing, you'll receive a license key by email. Enter it along with the email you purchased with to activate — this checks the key against our licensing server and issues a signed activation token tied to your Mac's hardware identifier, stored in your Keychain. No internet connection is required afterward for day-to-day use; the server is only contacted to activate or re-verify.

## Device limit

Each license key can be active on up to **2 machines** at a time. Activating on a third machine is rejected until you free up a slot. If you need to move a license (e.g. replacing a Mac), contact support (see [Support](/framecraft/legal/support/)) to reset your activations.

## Lost your key?

Use "Resend my license key" in the app's activation screen with your purchase email — it will be re-sent automatically if a matching license is found.
